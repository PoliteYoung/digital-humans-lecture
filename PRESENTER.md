# Presenter cheat sheet

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge → press **F** for full screen → press **S** for the speaker view
(notes, next slide, timer; drag that window to your laptop screen).

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| → / Space / mouse wheel down / left click | next (slide 7: first click reveals the 3DGS row) |
| ← / right click | back |
| S | speaker view |
| F / Esc | full screen / overview |
| B or . | black screen |

Videos: all clips are silent and play on their own in a loop while their slide is shown; leaving the slide pauses them. Clicking a video never changes the slide.

## PDF backup

Layout Landscape, Margins None, enable "Background graphics".
